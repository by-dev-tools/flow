ISSUE 1 · REDIRECT · Ambition ceiling
Brief says:
> "Add an "Undo" text link that appears for 5 seconds after a delete, restoring the item on click."
> "Where this pushes past the literal request: Nowhere — this satisfies exactly what was asked."

Gap: the brief explicitly declines to push past the literal request, but "whose moment" names the exact moment a user is anxious about a mistake they just made — a 5-second text link gives them almost no room to notice, decide, and act before the window closes, and nothing in the brief considers the reassurance/confidence dimension of "did that actually work."
Why it matters: an accidental-deletion recovery feature that's easy to miss defeats its own purpose; a user who misses the window once learns not to trust delete at all.
Suggested: extend the window or replace the timed disappearance with a dismissible toast, and consider whether the delete action itself should feel reversible-by-default rather than requiring the user to catch a 5-second link.
