### FB-0114 — A proportionality trigger is DECLARED, not measured — and a declaration nobody can reach is not a trigger

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Source type:** user direction (Ben, relayed by the orchestrator; resolves `dev-docs/handoffs/d1-prototype-first-gate.md` §9.2, deferred out of Phase 1 into Phase 2)

**What was said:** Offered three mechanisms for D1's "collapse on small surfaces" trigger — a size heuristic (files/lines), an explicit `mode: tiny`, or the agent's own judgment gated by the human at Clarify — Ben chose **explicit `mode: tiny`.** His reasoning, which generalizes past this decision: a size threshold is the [[FB-0010]] shape — *a tuned number that drifts and needs maintaining forever* — and judgment *cannot be pinned by a fixture*. `mode: tiny` already exists in the loop's vocabulary, it is **declared rather than inferred**, and it is **testable**.

**Synthesized rule:** When a mechanism decides *where a human gate sits*, prefer a **declaration** over a measurement or a judgment. A declaration is inspectable before the fact, pinnable by a fixture, and cannot drift; a tuned threshold needs permanent maintenance, and per-run judgment cannot be regression-tested at all.

**How to apply:**
- **The same rule applies one step over, and that is where it was nearly missed.** D1's trigger has two arms. The first draft of Phase 2 rejected a size threshold for the proportionality arm — then wrote *"the request is visual"* into the other arm: an un-pinnable judgment, on a deterministic engine that cannot evaluate it, one paragraph after rejecting un-pinnable judgment. It also cannot be inferred, since before anything is built there is no diff for `uiFilePatterns` to match. So it gets the identical treatment: the brief header declares **both** — `**Mode:** feature · **Surface:** visual` — and the engine accepts no free-floating flag, so no per-run vibe can reach the trigger.
- **A declaration nobody can reach is not a trigger.** The shipped definition of `tiny` is *"a 1–3 line bug fix the user explicitly asked you to 'just do'"* plus *"rarely the right call"*. Design work is essentially never a 1–3-line bug fix, so under that definition D1's only proportionality collapse was **undeclarable on exactly the work D1 exists to serve** — which would have rebuilt the ceremony D1 removes. Ben's decision chose the *mechanism*; checking that the mechanism was *reachable* was a separate obligation, and it was only caught at critique round 2.
- **The fix is a scoping statement, never a loosening.** A brief's `Mode` scopes the **pre-prototype phase only** and is **not inherited** by the technical plan, which declares its own. In a brief, `Mode: tiny` means *"this surface does not earn a prototype"*. The shipped `tiny` definition stays untouched at all five defining sites; the scoping sentence goes only to the three files a plan author actually reads.
- **Judgment still exists — it just moves to where it can be seen.** Someone still decides what to write in `Surface`. That is strictly better than an inferred verdict, but it is not airtight, so the resolved `path`, `pre_execution_gate` and `reasons[]` are printed in the gate hand-off: a mis-declaration becomes visible *before* the human commits, not after.
