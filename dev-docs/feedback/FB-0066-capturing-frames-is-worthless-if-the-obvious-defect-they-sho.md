### FB-0066: Capturing frames is worthless if the obvious defect they show is missed — an implementer must not read its own screenshots for sign-off, and "obvious" defects need a fixed checker no declared criterion gates

**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Source:** user correction (dogfood: a pull-down Settings pager was declared "verified" while its captured frames plainly showed the ambient background broken at the safe-area edges — white bands at the notch/home-indicator + a seam)

**What was said:** "You missed the obvious critical issues in the screenshots — that is the point of taking the screenshots and verifying the build." The frames were captured but two things let the defect through: (1) they were captured ad-hoc during Execute and eyeballed **by the implementing agent**, never routed through `/flow:verify-build` §5a's a11y-gated capture → fresh-context judge — an implementer glancing at its own output pattern-matches "looks like the app" instead of auditing it; (2) even §6's judges are criterion-scoped, so a defect no `Visual-walk` assertion named had no checker — the nearest dimension (`regression`) resolved `Unknown` with no baseline rather than `FAIL`. The same defect later recurred and was caught the right way by a disciplined on-sim edge-by-edge audit (root cause: a nested `NavigationStack` backing its scroll view with an opaque `systemBackground` that painted over the pager's single shared gradient, leaving it only in the safe-area strips) — concrete evidence that the disciplined audit surfaces what an implementer glance waves through.

**Synthesized rule:** A screenshot is captured to be *audited by an independent party*, not glanced at by its author. Two mechanical consequences: **(a) No self-certifying visual work.** An implementing agent may not claim a UI change "verified" from screenshots it took and read itself; ad-hoc Execute screenshots are for iterating, never sign-off — a visually-significant change routes through `/flow:verify-build` §5a (a11y-gated capture → fresh-context judge → frame-integrity checklist) for any visual verdict. This extends the MANDATORY-capture gate's spirit: zero captured frames ⇒ Unknown; implementer-eyeballed frames ⇒ not a verdict at all. **(b) "Obvious" defects need a fixed, criterion-independent checker.** A criterion-scoped judge only checks declared things; a defect no assertion named (broken safe-area background, seam, clipped text) needs a **must-pass checklist run on every frame regardless of the plan** — and because these are single-frame absolute properties, the verdict is FAIL, never the baseline-less Unknown the pairwise-layout rule uses (otherwise the gate only soft-blocks the exact defect it exists to catch). Force a literal per-edge/per-corner description *before* the verdict so a bare "looks fine" is structurally impossible. Same family as FB-0056 (a "this was verified" signal must be mechanically enforced with an untrusting default) and FB-0018 (the readiness signal must be a positive PASS, never absence-of-failure) — applied to *full-frame visual integrity*.

**Applies to:** `skills/verify-build/lib/frame-integrity-checklist.md` (new must-pass judge prompt), `skills/verify-build/lib/rubric.md` (VLM pre-pass note), `skills/verify-build/SKILL.md` §5a/§6/§7/§8, `findings-schema.json` + `findings-example.json` (`frame_integrity[]`), `render-report.py` (Frame integrity section), `docs/workflow.md` Step 8/9 (operator-discipline rule), `evals/run_frame_integrity_evals.py` + `ci.yml`; FB-0056 (enforce-with-untrusting-default sibling), FB-0018 (positive-PASS readiness), FB-0011 (ESCALATE on uncertainty), FB-0010 (fan-out — why frame-integrity is a top-level field, not a fourth `verdicts` key).
